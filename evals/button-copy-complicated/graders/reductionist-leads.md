The decision in the prompt is a small, reversible copy change that a clean A/B test can read.

PASS if the card places it in the Complicated domain (Clear is also acceptable), the Reductionist leads, the call is Go or Go with guardrails backed by an A/B test, and any systems concern appears as a guardrail rather than as a reason to stop.

FAIL if the card places it in the Complex or Chaotic domain, if the Systems thinker leads, if the call is Redesign or Don't because of speculative loops, or if there is no test.
