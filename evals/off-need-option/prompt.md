A partner asked us: "Members should be able to pause their membership from the app, without calling the front desk." Which option should we pick?

Fictional product: Stridewell, a booking app for fitness studios. The partner is Corepeak, a fictional chain with 6 of the 40 studios that use the app. The options:

1. Build an in-app pause with a date picker: about 3 weeks of work.
2. Add a "Call to pause" button that dials the studio: 1 day of work.
3. Email members a pause request form that staff process by hand: 2 days of work.
4. Wait until more studios ask for it.
