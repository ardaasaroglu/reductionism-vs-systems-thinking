Plan next quarter's referral work. Two things are on the table: fixing the invite link, and growth's ask to double the referral reward from $25 to $50.

Fictional product: Tealdock, a meal-kit app. 80,000 people pay us each month. 3% of them refer someone each month, each referrer sends about 2 invites, and 12% of invites become paying users. A paying user brings about $90 of margin over their lifetime. Since last month's app update, the invite link fails on one of the two mobile platforms, and about 30% of invites never open.
