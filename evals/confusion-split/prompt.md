Should we double our referral reward from $25 to $50?

Fictional product: Tealdock, a meal-kit app. 80,000 people pay us each month. 3% of them refer someone each month, each referrer sends about 2 invites that sign up, and 12% of those become paying users. A paying user brings about $90 of margin over their lifetime. Last month support found a handful of accounts that had referred themselves.
