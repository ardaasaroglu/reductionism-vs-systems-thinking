# Privacy

Reductionism vs Systems Thinking is a set of instructions for Claude: two skills and two subagents written in Markdown. It has no server, no account and no analytics.

## What it reads

* The decision you describe: text you type, paste or attach.
* Files you point it at, such as a metrics export or a spec.

It reads nothing else on your machine or in your account.

## Where your data goes

Only to Claude, the assistant you are already using, as part of your conversation. In Claude Code and Cowork, the two lens subagents are also Claude, reading the same decision. The plugin makes no network calls of its own and sends nothing outside the conversation, including to the author.

## What it writes

* The tradeoff card, in the conversation.
* In Claude Code, a file with the card, only if you ask for one.

It never edits your files. It stores nothing else and keeps no logs.

## Data handling by Claude

How Anthropic handles your conversations is set by your Claude plan and Anthropic's own privacy policy, not by this plugin.
